Wine Cellar

Welcome to Wine Cellar

Keep track of the bottles you own, what each one is worth, and — the part that matters — when to open it. Every wine carries a drinking window, and the app sorts your cellar into what to hold, what is ready and what to drink soon.

Everything you enter stays in this browser. Sign in with Google only if you want the same cellar on your other devices — no account is needed to use it.

Start Fresh opens the window for your first wine. To add several at once, press paste a list at the top of that window and paste the table Claude gives you when you ask it to look up value and drinking windows.

Load Sample Data fills the cellar with invented wines — one for every status, a few bottles already opened — so every figure on both tabs has something behind it.

Restore a Backup reads a Wine Cellar export — the JSON file the Back Up window writes.

Pairings

Meals and the bottles for them — planned ones first, soonest first, then the ones you have had. Press Pair a Meal to have Claude pick from your cellar, and press a line to rate how the pairing went.

History

What happened to each wine, newest first: added, changed by hand, looked up by Claude — what you took and what you left — opened, put back and undone. Each wine keeps its last 20 entries, and so does each opened bottle — its rating, note, date and where. Press a line to open it.

      One wine per line, pasted straight from a chat or a spreadsheet: the table Claude writes (Wine · Approx. value · Drink window · Verdict), or a CSV with a header row — Wine, Region, Grape, Bottles, Value, Window, Where, in any order.       Look Up with Claude

      With a key here, Look Up in the wine window and the paste window asks Claude to search the web for a wine’s value and drinking window, with its sources. Each lookup is a few cents from your API account. The key stays in this browser — it never syncs, and it is not in backups or share links. Every page on this web address can read this browser’s storage, so make a key just for this app in the Claude Console and give it a low monthly spend limit.

      Back Up & Restore

      One JSON file holding every wine, every opened bottle and your settings — a copy you keep, independent of this browser and of any account. If you don’t sign in to sync, clearing your browser data is the one thing that will lose your cellar.

      Every wine, and every opened bottle, as a spreadsheet — all of them, whatever the table is showing. It is a one-way door: the CSV is for reading elsewhere, and Restore reads the JSON above, never this.

      Restoring replaces everything in this browser. You are told what the file holds and asked to confirm first.

      Share a Read-Only Link

      Anyone opening this link sees the wines you pick, with no way to change them and no sign-in. Everything travels inside the link itself — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and it doesn't touch whatever they have saved in their own browser.

      Which Wines

      Nothing is deleted — this only decides what rides in the link. The figures in it are worked out from those wines alone, so a trimmed link shows a smaller cellar than yours, and whoever opens it is told so.

      What to Include

      Wines, bottles, values and windows ride either way: they are what the link exists to show. Notes, locations and what you paid are yours, which is why they stay behind unless you tick the box.

      Your Link

      This is a snapshot, frozen as it looks right now — later edits won't appear, so send a fresh link when the cellar changes. There is no way to withdraw one once sent, and whoever holds it can read everything inside it.

      Find

      Every wine — by its name, vintage, region, grape, where it is or its notes — and every opened bottle. A wine opens straight into its record.
